Our approach

Operational expertise,
where projects are truly won.

Most hospitality projects don’t fail on the idea, but on its execution: poorly designed workflows, an undersized kitchen, a P&L that slips, teams hired too late.

CHR Consulting bridges the gap between concept and real-world operations. We work on the ground — audit, design, opening, organisation, profitability — with one requirement: that the venue opens on time, holds its standards and makes money.

A method built on more than twenty years running premium, high-volume, multi-site operations, in France and abroad.

01

Best-effort commitment, results-driven culture

A clear commitment, a defined scope, and a focus on the concrete performance of your operation.

02

On the ground, not on PowerPoint

Openings, SOPs, recruitment, setting the standards: we support execution, not just strategy.

03

Numbers as a compass

Ratios, P&L, food & labour cost. Every decision is tied to its impact on profitability.

04

An industry network

Kitchen designers, equipment suppliers, craftsmen, talent: a ready-to-mobilise ecosystem, with a transparent commercial framework.
