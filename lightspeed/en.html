Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Do I need a consulting engagement to get equipped?

No. You can simply get equipped: we assess your needs, demo the solution, then install and configure the POS. Consulting is optional and can be added at any time.

I already have a Lightspeed POS. Can you help?

Yes. We audit your current setup, restructure it if needed and teach you to read your reports as ratios.

Who will I sign the contract with?

Your Lightspeed subscription and Lightspeed Payments are taken out directly with Lightspeed, who invoices you. Installation, configuration, training and support are delivered and invoiced by CHR Consulting, on quotation.

Who do I contact if there is a problem?

Us. CHR Consulting is your first point of contact for usage questions and everyday incidents, with a reply within one business day. If an issue goes beyond our level of support, we escalate it to Lightspeed support. Payment-related questions are handled directly by Lightspeed.

Can you get involved at the design stage of a project?

That is the ideal time. As part of a 360 Opening engagement, the POS is designed together with the flows, the menu and team organisation.

Where do you work?

On site in Paris and the Île-de-France region; remotely across France and Europe for configuration, training and follow-up.
